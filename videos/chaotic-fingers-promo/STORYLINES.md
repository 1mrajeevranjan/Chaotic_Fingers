# Storylines

Run was "no stops" (automation, no storyboard), so A was taken as recommended.

## A — Chaos, then the click (PICKED)

Device: life before and after. Form: object-to-object chain.
A Mac desktop falls apart under small hands, a cleaning cloth and a sleeping cat; one click on the
real Start Blocking button and the noise stops dead. Every later shot is entered through the thing
the last one clicked: button → menu bar icon → keycaps → Settings → the baby hand → the app icon.

- First 2 s: an email draft already flooding with "jjjjkkkk;;;;", key clatter loud, VO "Your toddler just found the keyboard."
- Beats: hook (draft floods) → tension (email sent, apps pop, space bar held) → reveal (dashboard, click, silence) → proof (modes, keys vanish, menu bar, shortcuts, recovery hold, failsafe, settings, privacy) → payoff (real baby hand on the keys, draft stays clean) → claim (Screen on. Inputs off.) → end card (hand becomes the app icon, CTA).
- Look: light macOS — #F5F5F7 ground, the app's own key-pattern textures, Inter, system blue rationed.
- Sound: frantic key clatter under the chaos that cuts to dead silence on the click; catalog bed enters after the silence; af_heart narration.
- Why: a stranger recognises the toddler-on-lap moment instantly, and the click-to-silence turn is the product's whole promise in one frame.

## B — One video call

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take.
A parent on a work call with a toddler on their lap; the camera never cuts as small hands reach for
the keys, the parent locks it from the menu bar, the call carries on. Features surface as the call goes.

## C — The keyboard argues back

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Giant kinetic type made of garbage keystrokes ("asdfjkl;;;") on colour fields, cut to the music,
until the click turns the noise into clean lines naming each feature.
